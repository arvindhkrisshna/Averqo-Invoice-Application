Averqo sends invoices and quotes from your own email account, with the PDF attached. It's free: it uses your email provider's SMTP server, like any email app.

Using Gmail? Turn on 2-step verification for your Google account, then create an App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste it below. Your normal Gmail password won't work.

The password is stored in your Codespace's database and is never shown again. To stop sending, clear the SMTP server and save.
